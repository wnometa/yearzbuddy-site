# Yearzbuddy-Website
Official website for Yearzbuddy, a Nigerian record label, music distribution and publishing company. Distribution for independent artists, the Artist Savings Vault, and a blog of artist and entertainment stories on Medium.

Yearzbuddy is an independent Nigerian record label, distribution and
publishing company founded in 2024. This site covers:

- Music distribution for independent artists
- The Artist Savings Vault
- A blog for artists and entertainers on Medium
- Information for investors
