The Naija sound

Nigerian music has shaped the world, from the highlife bands of the 1950s to today's Afrobeats stars. Here is the family tree Yearzbuddy artists grow from.

Global

Afrobeats

Today's Lagos-born pop sound: bright rhythms, big hooks, and a worldwide dance floor.

1970s

Afrobeat

Fela Kuti's mix of highlife, jazz and funk, with long grooves and sharp political lyrics.

Roots

Highlife

Guitar-led, horn-rich dance music that soundtracked independence-era parties.

Yoruba

Fuji

Percussion-heavy and powered by the talking drum, a staple of every owambe.

Yoruba

Juju

Smooth guitars and layered drums, made famous by King Sunny Adé and I.K. Dairo.
